Campus Issue Management Platform

One Platform for a Better Campus

The MVGR Campus Issue Portal provides a structured way for students to report campus-related problems and follow their resolution from submission to closure.

01

Easy Reporting

Submit classroom, laboratory, hostel, or network issues in seconds with exact location details and evidence attachments.

02

Transparent Tracking

Follow live lifecycle updates from initial submission through verification, assignment, and verified resolution.

03

Faster Resolution

Automated routing directs reported problems directly to the responsible departmental coordinators and campus maintenance squads.

04

Better Communication

Exchange direct follow-up inquiries, progress notes, and receive automated notifications at every workflow milestone.

Student Resolution Lifecycle

How It Works

A structured three-step workflow designed to resolve student concerns with speed and accountability.

01

REPORT

Submit a campus issue with its category, location, description, and supporting information.

02

TRACK

Follow the status of your reported issue as it moves through the resolution process.

03

RESOLVE

The responsible team reviews, works on, and updates the issue until resolution.
